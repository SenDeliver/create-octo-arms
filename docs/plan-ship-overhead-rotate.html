Ship Overhead and Turn

2.0 line, branch v1.21.1-v2, 1.21.1 only (Ship Grab needs Sable). Two additions to Ship Grab: hold a Ship Overhead to clear the view, and Turn a held Ship with the mouse, the Actuators visibly doing it. The feel follows Simulated's Creative Physics Staff (rotate mode): yaw about world up, pitch about the camera's horizontal right axis, smooth, camera still.

Behaviour

server Overhead

  • The anchor uses a yaw-only basis (horizontal forward, right, world up), so pitch does not move the Ship: the wearer looks down or forward under the hull. Yaw still turns it with the wearer, like the in-front hold.
  • Height: the held face goes up until the hull's lowest world point, read from the Sable boundingBox() at the current orientation, sits OVERHEAD_CLEAR = 0.75 above the eye. Horizontally it sits OVERHEAD_FORWARD ≈ 0.5 ahead of the eye.
  • The anchor is capped at Reach − SHIP_INSIDE from the Rig body. A hull too tall to clear goes as high as Reach allows.
  • The move is the existing gain (feed + P term) toward the new aim, so it flies up smoothly.

server Turn

  • The Held gets a user quaternion. want = rotY(−yawDelta) · user · start. The pivot is the midpoint of the two tips (first the held face; see Turn by hand), so the tips don't slide.
  • Each input delta is applied in the wearer's view frame: yaw premultiplied about world Y, pitch premultiplied about the horizontal right of the current yaw. This is what the Physics Staff does.
  • Packet RigGrabPacket.turn(dYaw, dPitch) in degrees. The server clamps it to ±30° per packet and drops more than 2 packets a tick. Ships only, ignored for mobs.
  • The turn stays after Set Down and Throw: the Ship is let go in its pose as it is.

client Input

  • A new client mixin on MouseHandler.turnPlayer (or the accumulated delta): while turning, it swallows the look delta and hands it to ShipTurn. Mouse sensitivity and invert are applied the vanilla way. Deltas are summed and sent once a tick.
  • Use with the Grab key down while a Ship is held is consumed (no vanilla use, no Far Use).
  • The turn stops when the key, the screen or the hold goes away.

visual Actuators turn it

  • At the Grab the server picks a face tangent: the in-face axis closest to the wearer's right. The two tips grip ship-local points at the face centre ± 0.4 along it.
  • The state packet carries the tangent as a Direction, appended at the end. ActuatorRenderer.holdPoint maps the ship-local points through renderPose, replacing today's world-space b.out(side).
  • Roll lifts one tip and drops the other, and yaw twists them. In Overhead the tips reach up.

HUD Gauge and hints

  • The distance tick column gets an Overhead cap past the farthest tick: a small gap, then an up-chevron glyph in its own colour. It lights when Overhead and is dim otherwise. Shown for Ship holds only.
  • While turning, a small rotate glyph sits by the crosshair. No words.
  • Ship hold hint line (first 2 holds): adds the Turn key combo. Lang keys in all 15 locales.

risk Watch

  • A long hull pitched while Overhead swings its far end down, possibly into the wearer or the terrain. Sable collides, and Hold.far releases it if it can't follow.
  • An Overhead Ship rising into a ceiling stays pressed against it. That's acceptable.
  • The frozen camera must never stick: every exit path (key up, screen, hold end, death, dimension change) restores look.

Turn by hand

Owner feedback after the first build: the tips stayed where they took hold, so a big Ship turned half round left them reaching through the hull. Now the two Actuators turn it the way two hands would: each holds its own point, pushes and pulls during a stroke, and regrips hand over hand.

server Tips and pivot (rig/ShipHands)

  • Each tip holds its own point on the hull in plot space; at the Grab the two sit 0.4 either side of the clicked face centre, as before.
  • The hold and the Turn pivot on the midpoint of the tips (h.local), so during a stroke the tips stay on their points and visibly push and pull (yaw), lift and lower (roll), tilt (pitch).
  • Stroke limit: more than 45° of Turn since it took hold, its surface more than 60° away from its shoulder, more than 1 block farther from the shoulder than it was, or nearer than 0.35.
  • Regrip: the tip furthest past its limit lets go and reaches for 6 ticks to the first hull block on the line from its shoulder to the hull middle (0.4 to its own side first, then the middle), a plot-space ray against this Ship's blocks only. The new point must face its shoulder within 50° and be 0.25 away from the old one, else the tip keeps its point and rests 10 ticks. The other waits until it holds again: never both off.

server No jump, no drift, rate

  • The pivot moves only when a tip takes hold. That tick the hold gets a correction that keeps the new pivot exactly where the last body aim had it, so the commanded pose is the same. The correction is kept in the hold's basis (camera in front, yaw basis Overhead).
  • The correction settles back to the hold anchor: 70% kept each tick, at most 0.25 a tick. Without it every regrip would keep the swing of the stroke and the hull would roll round the wearer and close in. The wheel distance (rel) and the gauge stay put.
  • Turn input goes into a bank (at most 90° each axis) drained at 9° a tick with both tips holding and 4.5° while one reaches, so a flick cannot spend both strokes in one tick and the Turn total is what the mouse asked.
  • The far rule measures the nearest holding tip, never the moving midpoint.

client Wire and drawing

  • ActuatorStatePacket appends a Tip for the body-left and body-right Actuator: the point (and, while reaching, the old point) as floats relative to the held block, plus the reach ticks, −1 when holding. Sent on every let-go and take-hold, and on start of tracking. Registrar 13.
  • Holding: the tip sits on its point through the Ship's renderPose(pt). Reaching: it eases from the old to the new point in ship space, lifts off toward its shoulder in a small arc, the gripper opens, then clamps on. Watching clients draw the same.

verify Tests

  • JUnit ShipHandsTest: the stroke limit, regrip choice and order, no better point keeps the old one, the pivot change keeps the body aim exactly and settles back, the bank, and a 180° Turn of a 3×3×3 hull in plain math.
  • Harness shipgrab VT: 12 moves of 15° right. The Turn totals 180°, each side regrips, never both off, both tips end nearer the eye than the hull centre, the pose never jumps, the hull stays in front, and the wearer and the watcher are told the same tips.
