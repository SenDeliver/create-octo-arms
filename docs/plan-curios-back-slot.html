Curios Back Slot for Octo Arms 1.8.0

Issue #17. Both lines: 1.21.1 (NeoForge, Curios 9.5.1) and 1.20.1 (Forge 47, Curios 5.x). Source: v2 slice 7 (3a00ceb, ADR 0006) backported, plus the gameplay rules below. Minor bump: client + server change, registrar 8 → 9.

1. Rules

A Rig in the Curios back slot is a Rig, not armor. Everything that makes it a Rig stays: Air, Reach, Rig enchantments (Far Reach, Thrift, Swift, Silent Step), Capacity, Rig Settings, durability wear. Nothing that makes it armor applies: no armor value, toughness, knockback resistance; Protection, Thorns and the other armor enchantments are idle there. The chest slot keeps today's behaviour unchanged.

6. Risks

  • risk Create 6.0.8 (1.20.1) may lack the curio air supplier: own supplier, verified by B0.
  • risk Curios event spam on isItemValid during menu hovering: refusal message throttled per player.
  • risk Client-side identity of the worn stack changes on every inventory sync: the client guard compares slots, not objects; the server guard decides.
  • risk Old client (registrar 8) cannot join a 1.8.0 server. Changelog "Compatibility" line.
