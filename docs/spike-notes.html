2. Movement model as built

Held

Each of the four Tentacles owns one horizontal quadrant around the body (bbox centre). A quadrant has a Grip when an exposed face of a full-collision block lies in it, faces the body and has a point within Reach. Held = at least 2 quadrants with a Grip. This gives: floors, walls, ceilings, pillar sides and underwater faces all hold; a straight edge holds with the 2 quadrants behind it; an outside corner holds with 1 (so you stop there).

The drawn Grip of a slot stays planted until it leaves Reach, its quadrant or its face; then it picks the face point nearest a spread target (0.45·Reach out on the slot diagonal).

Motion (client)

Heading = look vector (with pitch) × W/S + camera-left × A/D + world ±Y for Space/Shift, normalised to 5.6 b/s. The move is collided (Entity.collideBoundingBox), then clamped: full move if still Held, else bisection; also tries the move with one axis dropped and keeps the one with most progress along the Heading (slides along gap edges, goes straight down a cliff wall at Reach). No input = zero velocity. Vanilla jump/sneak/walk input is zeroed in MovementInputUpdateEvent after reading; abilities.flying forced false (with onUpdateAbilities()).

Server

Never trusts the client state: Rig must be worn and alive; NaN/Inf motion or Grip → Stow. Each client Grip is checked (block full-collision, face exposed, point on that face, within Reach + 0.75). Only with ≥2 valid Grips: setDeltaMovement (speed clamped to 8·1.05 b/s), resetFallDistance, flight modifier on, flying=false. Claims of Active/Setting Down without 2 valid Grips: 10-tick grace, then forced Falling (sticky until the server itself sees 2 valid Grips). Every server tick the stored Grips are revalidated; no packet for 10 ticks → Falling + modifier off. hurtMarked is never set; no isChangingDimension trick.

5. Open issues

  • At full Reach all Grips collapse onto the nearest point (all four lines straight up under a ceiling). Closed in phase 6: the gait keeps four spread Grips (min pairwise 1.7–2.0 at Copper max depth, 2.6 Netherite), see phase-notes.html, Phase 6.
  • Setting Down over a deep pit: decided and built in phase 2 (lets go at the lowest Held point), see phase-notes.html.
  • Untested surfaces: ladders/vines (vanilla handleOnClimbable clamps velocity), cobweb, soul sand, honey, slabs/stairs as Grips (non-full blocks are not Grips at all), entities in the way, other players riding.
  • Elytra suppression only by zeroed jump input; no elytra test.
  • Client-side face scan is 13³ block states per tick plus ~30 Held checks for the bisection; fine for one player, not profiled. Server cost per tick is only Grip validation.
  • Hacked client can still move anywhere 2 real Grips exist (inherent to adr/0001); it cannot float without them (controls above).
  • Air, Crawl, Netherite Reach, Rig Settings, gait, sounds: not in the spike.
  • Harness noise, not Rig bugs: a NeoForge neoforge-client.new.tmp.toml NoSuchFileException on the first client start with a fresh run/config; vanilla logs moved too quickly 3–5 times after every RCON tp/kill (analyzer matches them to the command); after a dimension change the client falls a few ticks in not-yet-loaded chunks (Rig Stowed); peaceful heals fall damage within seconds, so use STRACE hp, not a later Health read.
